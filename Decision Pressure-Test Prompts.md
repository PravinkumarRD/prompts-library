# Decision Pressure-Test Prompts

## Short, Action-Oriented and Natural Prompts for Executives

These prompts help executive leaders use Microsoft Copilot to challenge assumptions, evaluate risks, assess scenarios, and improve decision quality before committing to a plan.

---

# Topic 1: Pressure-Test a Decision

## 1. SWOT Analysis

> Act as a Strategy Advisor. Analyse my proposal using a SWOT framework, highlighting strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, and threats. Provide recommendations for improving the proposal. Use an objective and strategic tone.

---

## 2. Scenario Analysis

> Act as a Business Strategy Advisor. Evaluate my proposal using best-case, most likely, and worst-case scenarios, highlighting potential outcomes, business impact, and recommended actions. Use a practical and data-driven tone.

---

## 3. Risk Assessment

> Act as a Risk Management Advisor. Review my proposal and identify strategic, financial, operational, and reputational risks, along with mitigation recommendations. Use a balanced and analytical tone.

---

## 4. Decision Readiness Review

> Act as an Executive Decision Advisor. Assess whether sufficient information exists to support this decision, highlighting assumptions, information gaps, risks, and additional data that should be considered. Use an objective and professional tone.

---

## 5. Executive Decision Summary

> Act as an Executive Strategy Consultant. Analyse this business decision, summarise potential benefits, risks, dependencies, and expected outcomes, and recommend whether to proceed, delay, or reconsider. Use a concise and strategic tone.

---

# Topic 2: Devil's Advocate Review

## 6. Challenge My Plan

> Act as an Independent Board Advisor. Review my proposal and challenge its assumptions, risks, and expected outcomes. Highlight weaknesses and recommend improvements before implementation. Use a candid and objective tone.

---

## 7. Find Blind Spots

> Act as a Strategic Risk Advisor. Analyse my plan and identify blind spots, overlooked risks, unintended consequences, and stakeholder concerns that could affect success. Use a critical and constructive tone.

---

## 8. Why Might This Fail?

> Act as a Devil's Advocate. Explain why this initiative might fail, identifying operational, financial, customer, technology, and people-related risks. Suggest actions to reduce these risks. Use a practical and challenging tone.

---

## 9. Stakeholder Opposition Analysis

> Act as a Stakeholder Strategy Advisor. Analyse my proposal from the perspective of employees, customers, executives, shareholders, and partners. Highlight likely concerns, objections, and recommended responses. Use an objective and balanced tone.

---

## 10. Pre-Mortem Analysis

> Act as a Business Transformation Advisor. Assume this initiative failed one year after implementation. Explain the most likely reasons for failure and recommend preventive actions that should be taken now. Use a critical and forward-looking tone.

# Decision Quality Formula

## Evaluate → Challenge → Improve

A strong decision review should:

1. Evaluate the proposal objectively.
2. Challenge assumptions and blind spots.
3. Improve the proposal before implementation.

---

# Universal Decision Pressure-Test Prompt

> Act as an Executive Decision Advisor. Review my proposal, identify risks, assumptions, opportunities, stakeholder concerns, and implementation challenges. Evaluate alternative outcomes and recommend actions that would increase the probability of success. Use an objective and strategic tone.

# Executive Tips

### Before Making the Decision

Ask:
- What are the benefits?
- What are the risks?
- What assumptions am I making?

### Before Approving the Decision

Ask:
- What could go wrong?
- Who might disagree?
- What am I missing?

### Before Implementation

Ask:
- What must be true for success?
- What should I monitor?
- What are the early warning signs?

This transforms AI from a content generator into a decision-support partner.
